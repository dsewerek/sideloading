# 1 - AltStore/SideStore(+LiveContainer)

This method is the main method to sideload as of today.
I am going to try to summarize everything that has happened from the 25th of September to today - the 5th of October.
On the 25th of September, u/MightyWolf39 [posted the fact that he got permanently banned from (the 1st method of) sideloading](https://www.reddit.com/r/sideloaded/comments/1wq3v5m/wow_the_banning_of_personalfree_apple_id_for/), and later [u/VENEANCE_](https://www.reddit.com/user/VENEANCE_/) [posted the fact that he also got permanently banned from (the 1st method of) sideloading](https://www.reddit.com/r/sideloaded/comments/1wuve6u/have_i_met_the_ban_hammer_sidestore_sideload/). (Please read at least both posts, they're short!) 
Later on, on the 4th of October, the leader&creator of LiveContainer(a popular tool widely used in the 1st method of sideloading) got falsely shadow-banned from GitHub, and therefore hiding the LiveContainer repository, possibly deleting it. [(Please read his X post here!)](https://x.com/khanhduytran0/status/2106620091161018400) GitHub has asked Duy Tran to delete his PojavLauncher(an Android, and later iOS app allowing you to run Java Minecraft on iOS&Android) repository for no aparent reason.
